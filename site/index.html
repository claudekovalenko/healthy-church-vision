Healthy Church in LA

The goal: establish healthy, Bible-centered churches that reach Los Angeles.

“Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her.” Ephesians 5:25

1Keep Jesus at the center

  • Make Jesus the focus of everything we do.
  • Be kind to one another. John 13:35

2Teach the Word faithfully

  • Have a mature, capable teacher lead the teaching. Titus 1:9
  • Study the Bible deeply and regularly.
  • Ask what the text actually says. No personal interpretations.

3Establish godly leadership

  • Appoint elders: proven, mature men who teach and oversee. 1 Tim 3:1–7
  • Appoint deacons to serve practical needs. 1 Tim 3:8–13
  • Involve older, mature believers, such as Titus’s parents.
  • Keep leadership complementarian. Elders are qualified men.
  • Train younger men toward eldership.

4Practice baptism and communion with care

  • A pastor baptizes when possible.
  • An elder serves communion when possible.

5Build healthy families

  • Husbands lead their homes spiritually. Eph 5:25; 6:4
  • Each home prays and reads Scripture together.

6Gather as a church

  • Meet in a building outside the home for worship, the Word, and the ordinances.

7Reach Los Angeles

  • Plant healthy churches across LA.
  • Equip members to live on mission where they live and work.
  • Reach people through social media.
  • Reach students at universities.
Every point is meant to be tested against Scripture.
