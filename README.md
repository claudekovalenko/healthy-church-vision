# A Vision for Healthy Church in Los Angeles

> "Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her." (Ephesians 5:25)

This is a simple picture of the kind of church I want to see established, and how I hope it reaches LA.

---

## 1. Jesus at the Center

- Hearts that burn for Jesus. He is the focus of everything believers do.
- Worship, teaching, family life, and outreach all point back to Him.
- People are kind to each other. Love is visible (John 13:35).

## 2. The Word Has Great Weight

The church is doctrinally sound because the Bible is taught clearly and studied deeply.

- **A capable teacher.** Someone more mature in the faith helps teach the Word, someone "able to teach" (1 Timothy 3:2) who holds firm to sound doctrine (Titus 1:9).
- **Regular, deep study.** The church studies Scripture consistently, not only on Sundays.
- **What does the text actually say?** Bible study leaders don't bring in their own personal readings. They ask:
  1. What does the passage say?
  2. What did it mean to its first readers?
  3. How does the rest of Scripture shed light on it?
  4. How do we obey it today?
- Leaders use reliable tools (good translations, sound commentaries) and stay accountable to the elders for what they teach.

## 3. Godly, Mature Leadership

The church is under healthy oversight, with godly men leading.

- **Elders** shepherd, teach, and oversee the church (1 Timothy 3:1–7; Titus 1:5–9; 1 Peter 5:1–4).
- **Deacons** serve the practical needs of the church so elders can focus on prayer and the Word (Acts 6:1–6; 1 Timothy 3:8–13).
- **Older leadership is involved.** Mature believers who have proven themselves over time, such as Titus's parents, would be a real strength.
- **Complementarian.** Men and women are equal in worth and both are fully gifted for ministry. The office of elder is held by qualified men.

### Can a younger man be an elder?

"Elder" (*presbyteros*) literally means "older man," and the role assumes maturity. Scripture doesn't set a minimum age, though. It focuses on qualifications:

- Proven character: above reproach, faithful to his wife, self-controlled, hospitable, gentle (1 Timothy 3:2–3)
- Able to teach and to defend sound doctrine (Titus 1:9)
- Manages his own household well (1 Timothy 3:4–5)
- **Not a recent convert** (1 Timothy 3:6)
- Respected by outsiders (1 Timothy 3:7)

Timothy was young and still led with authority ("let no one despise you for your youth," 1 Timothy 4:12). So a younger man *can* qualify. The question is proven maturity, not age alone. The wise path is to start with older, tested men as elders and train younger men toward that role.

## 4. Ordinances Done with Care

- **Baptism:** performed by a pastor when possible.
- **Communion:** the elements administered by an elder when possible.

(Note: in the New Testament, "pastor," "elder," and "overseer" all describe the same office; see Acts 20:17, 28 and 1 Peter 5:1–2. So we could simply say the elders/pastors oversee both ordinances.)

## 5. Healthy Families

A big part of this vision is healthy families being established and coming out of the church.

- **Each home as a little church.** Families pray, read Scripture, and worship together in the home.
- **The husband and father leads spiritually.** He takes responsibility to lead his household in the Word and in prayer, loving his wife as Christ loved the church (Ephesians 5:25–28; 6:4; Deuteronomy 6:6–7).
- Healthy marriages and children raised in the Lord become a witness to the city.

## 6. A Gathered Church with a Building

- Personally, I like the church meeting in a building outside the home.
- Homes are where faith is lived out daily. The building is where the whole church family gathers under its elders for worship, the Word, and the ordinances.
- Both matter: **strong homes** that feed into **a strong gathered church**.

## 7. Reaching Los Angeles

In my perfect world, this is how LA is reached:

- **Healthy churches all over LA** that plant and build up other healthy churches.
- **Missional guidance** for believers, helping them live on mission where they already are.
- **Healthy families** coming out of these churches.
- **Missionally minded members** who reach people in their own context: their jobs, neighborhoods, and friendships.
- **Under healthy leadership and oversight** by godly men.

### Where I see myself serving

- **Social media:** sharing the gospel and sound teaching with people across LA.
- **Universities:** reaching students and connecting them to healthy local churches.

---

## Marks of Health: Summary Checklist

- [ ] Jesus is the center of everything
- [ ] The Word is taught and studied deeply and faithfully
- [ ] Bible study seeks what the text actually says
- [ ] Qualified elders and deacons are in place
- [ ] Older, mature leaders are involved
- [ ] Complementarian leadership structure
- [ ] Baptism and communion overseen by pastors/elders
- [ ] Husbands lead their homes spiritually, and each home is a little church
- [ ] People are kind and love one another
- [ ] Members are on mission in their own context
- [ ] The church is planting and strengthening other healthy churches across LA
